          Where this comes from

          A bot reads each company's own job board every hour, mostly through the public APIs behind Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and similar systems. Internships are titles that say intern, co-op or student. New grad roles are engineering, robotics and AI/ML titles that say new grad, early career, entry level, junior or university graduate, plus roles whose descriptions welcome recent graduates or ask for 0 years of experience.

          How forecasts work

          A company's expected date is the day its first summer internship appeared last year, a year on. "By" means it was already open when we started watching, so it opened that day or earlier.

          Open and closed

          A posting closes when its board stops listing it. Large drops wait an hour for a second look, so a board that briefly fails to load doesn't close everything. Built with phlux.
