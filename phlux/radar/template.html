          Where this comes from

          A bot reads each company's own job board every hour, mostly through the public APIs behind Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and similar systems, and keeps every title it has seen. Internships are titles that say intern, co-op or student.

          How forecasts work

          A company's expected date is the day its first summer internship appeared last year, a year on. "By" means it was already open when we started watching, so it opened that day or earlier.

          Open and closed

          A posting closes when its board stops listing it. Large drops wait an hour for a second look before they count, so a board that briefly fails to load doesn't close everything. Built by phlux.
